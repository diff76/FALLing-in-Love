Subject: the sunken colonnade terrace in front of the main building as a clay diorama from a three-quarter aerial angle, the SOCIAL moment: two rows of tall white round columns carrying the pale-stone and teal-glass building above, with an elevated glass bridge wing overhead; between the columns a long rectangular dark reflecting pool edged in grey stone with reeds and three small fountain jets; brick-red pavers around it. The focus is the tables: café tables with cream and amber parasols, small coffee cups, carafes and plates of finger food, faceless clay figures in autumn clothes sitting in pairs and small groups, leaning toward each other in conversation, a few standing with cups by the pool. A jazz trio is present but small and off to one side, not yet the centre. On the west side a natural grassy slope with trees rises to the trail hill above (no walls). Late, low golden light, long soft shadows.
