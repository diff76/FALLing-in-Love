Subject: the campus arrival as a clay diorama from a high three-quarter angle looking north: in the foreground the small dark gatehouse with red-and-white barrier arms and a zebra crossing; beyond it the large bare-earth sports ground used as a car park, ochre clay, with a few neat rows of small cars and a plain iron fence along its edge; along the east side a grey paver strip holding one cream peaked welcome tent with a desk, a small cream shuttle bus just arrived, and faceless clay figures stepping off and walking to the tent; between the car park and the tent strip the first dark wooden sleepers of a forest stair disappear up into the trees. Dense autumn woodland rises behind, and at the top edge the white main building and the chapel's teal glass are just visible above the trees.
