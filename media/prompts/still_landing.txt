Subject: the campus arrival as a clay diorama from a raised three-quarter angle looking north-east, laid out exactly as the anchor map: the large bare-earth sports ground used as a car park fills the centre, ochre clay with a few neat rows of small cars and a plain iron fence. Along the car park's EAST SIDE runs a grey paver strip with orange parking lines. The main gate sits at the near, south end of that strip, at the SIDE of the car park — a small pale guard booth with a green roof, red-and-white barrier arms, a zebra crossing and a dark stone gate sign (blank) opening onto the city street — so the gate faces the strip, not the stair. The strip runs north past one cream peaked welcome tent with a desk and a festive small tour bus (glossy cream with a coral, amber and mint side stripe and little pennant flags) where figures step off and walk to the tent. At the far north end of the strip, the forest stair of dark wooden sleepers begins and winds up into the pines. Dense autumn woodland rises behind, and above the trees the white main building and the chapel's teal glass and cross tower are just visible. Faceless clay figures, sparse. No text.
