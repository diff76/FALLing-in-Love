Subject: the campus arrival as a clay diorama. Take the layout, framing and mood of the first reference image for everything on the campus side: the bare-earth sports ground car park with its rows of small cars parked exactly as in that reference (the cars face the fence side, not the gate), the narrow grey paver strip with orange parking lines along the ground's right side with no kerb or edging between the two surfaces, the cream welcome tent with orange-sweatshirt staff and the festive tour bus with its right-hand door toward the tent, and the wooden stair curving up from the top end of the strip into the pines toward the buildings at the top edge. Take the gate ONLY from the second reference image: at the bottom right, a campus road leaving the city street through the main gate, where — for a vehicle driving in — a substantial pale guard booth with a green roof stands on the LEFT of the road, about twice the size of a car, the red-and-white barrier arm hinged beside the booth and swinging open toward the campus, a zebra crossing, and an iron fence running from the gate along the street. Dense autumn woodland around.
