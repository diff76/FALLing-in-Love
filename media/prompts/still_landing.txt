Subject: the campus arrival as a clay diorama from a raised three-quarter angle looking north, laid out like the anchor overview and the photographs. The city street runs along the bottom-right. From it a two-lane campus road enters at the bottom right through the main gate: a small pale guard booth with a green roof on the road's left, red-and-white barrier arms spanning the road, a zebra crossing, and an iron fence continuing on both sides of the gate along the street — the gate and its barrier face the way the road runs, straight INTO the campus toward the sports ground and the buildings, so vehicles come off the big street, pass the booth, and drive up into the campus. That road runs north along the EAST side of the site, then bends left in front of the chapel and ends where the top of the wooden stair arrives. To the LEFT (west) of the road lies the narrow grey paver strip with orange parking lines, and beyond it the LARGE bare-earth sports ground used as a car park — ochre clay, long rows of small cars, an iron fence along its far sides — much bigger than the strip, with no kerb or edging between the two surfaces. On the strip: one cream peaked welcome tent with a desk staffed by figures in orange crew-neck sweatshirts, and the festive tour bus (glossy cream with a coral, amber and mint side stripe and little pennant flags) parked with its right-hand door toward the tent, guests stepping off. From the north end of the strip the forest stair of dark wooden sleepers climbs the wooded slope in a gentle, clearly CURVED S-line (not a straight run) between pines and rounded autumn shrubs, small easels with blank pictures along one side, and arrives at the top in the gap between the chapel and the main building where the bending road meets it. Dense autumn woodland; above the trees the white main building with its teal glass and the chapel's cross tower.
