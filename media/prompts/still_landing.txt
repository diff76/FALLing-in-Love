Subject: the campus arrival as a clay diorama, laid out like the anchor overview but framed as a closer, lower three-quarter view of the arrival area only: the camera sits over the bottom-right of the anchor's world, looking north-west, so the frame holds the gate, the car park, the welcome strip and the foot of the stair, with the main building and chapel only partly visible at the top edge. The bare-earth sports ground is a WIDE landscape rectangle, much longer from left to right than from front to back, filled with long rows of small cars; along its right (east) side the narrow grey paver strip with orange parking lines, with no kerb or edging between the two surfaces; on the strip the cream peaked welcome tent staffed by figures in orange crew-neck sweatshirts and the festive tour bus parked with its right-hand door toward the tent. The main gate is at the bottom right where the campus road leaves the city street: for a vehicle driving IN, the guard booth stands on the LEFT of the road — a substantial pale booth with a green roof, about twice the size of a car — and the red-and-white barrier arm is hinged beside the booth and swings open toward the campus; an iron fence continues from the gate along the street. The campus road runs from the gate up the east side and bends left in front of the chapel. From the TOP (north) edge of the car park two routes leave: the forest stair of dark wooden sleepers curving gently up through the pines toward the gap between chapel and main building, and beside it a footpath that climbs the wooded slope toward the trail lawn — neither of them from the right side of the ground. Dense autumn woodland around.
