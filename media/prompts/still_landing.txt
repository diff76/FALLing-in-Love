Subject: the campus arrival as a clay diorama from a raised three-quarter angle looking north. The LARGE bare-earth sports ground used as a car park dominates the frame — ochre clay, several long rows of small cars, a plain iron fence — at least four times the area of the paved strip beside it. Along the car park's EAST edge runs a NARROW grey paver strip with orange parking lines, about one fifth as wide as the car park. The main gate is on that east side and is turned sideways: the small pale guard booth with a green roof, the red-and-white barrier arms and the zebra crossing face EAST toward the city street that runs along the right edge of the frame, so cars turn in from the right, drive through the gate onto the strip, and the gate never faces the camera or the stair. Beside the strip stands one cream peaked welcome tent with a desk staffed by figures in orange crew-neck sweatshirts, and a festive small tour bus (glossy cream with a coral, amber and mint side stripe and little pennant flags), parked with its right-hand passenger door toward the tent, guests stepping off. At the far north end of the strip the forest stair of dark wooden sleepers begins and winds up into the pines. Dense autumn woodland rises behind; above the trees the white main building and the chapel's teal glass and cross tower are just visible.
