# Scene prompts (Track 2)

`style-preamble.txt` is prepended verbatim to every still. Each `still_<scene>.txt` describes ONE
scene as a clay diorama vignette (3:2, centred focal subject, headroom) and cites the campus facts in
`docs/CAMPUS_SPATIAL_REFERENCE.md`. Reference images passed with `--image`: the approved anchor
(`media/anchor/anchor-a.png`) plus the listed campus photographs.

Dive prompts (`dive_<scene>.txt`) and connector prompts (`conn_<i>.txt`) follow the scroll-world
templates and are generated by `media/scripts/02-stills.sh` … once the stills are approved.
