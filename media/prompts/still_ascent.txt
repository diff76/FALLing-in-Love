Subject: a short, narrow wooden-sleeper forest stair winding up a wooded slope in a gentle S-curve, as a clay diorama seen from low on the stair looking up, the treads dark brown timber with no handrails, red-barked pines and rounded clay shrubs pressing close on both sides, autumn leaves on the steps; along the left side a single vertical row of small framed picture panels (blank, no text) leaning on the slope; at the top of the stair, framed by the trees, the gap between the chapel's teal-green glass volume on the right and the main building's white colonnade on the left opens onto the upper ground in afternoon sun. Two faceless clay figures climb, spaced apart.
