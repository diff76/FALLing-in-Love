Subject: the wide upper lawn as a clay diorama from a gentle aerial angle: a softly domed green-ochre grass hill, never flat, its crown higher than the fountain terrace beside it, with a grey stone-slab walkway curving over the rise toward the main building's entrance, dressed as a garden party: a few red-brick paved rectangles set into the grass holding small wooden easels with blank picture boards and one red wooden bench; clusters of cream, coral and butter-yellow balloons on slim posts; strings of small bunting flags between slender poles; a photo zone made of a free-standing frame wrapped in autumn flowers and balloons with two figures posing; one small draped table where a figure stamps cards for a short line of guests; a little refreshment cart; tall pines on the left; NO monument stone, NO stele, NO glass display case; the long three-storey glass facade of the main building across the back with its elevated bridge wing to the right, and the L-shaped concrete library at the far left behind the pines. Faceless clay figures stroll in ones and twos, one pair taking a photo. Quiet, spacious, breathing room.
