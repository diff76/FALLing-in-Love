Subject: the wide upper lawn as a clay diorama from a gentle aerial angle: a softly domed green-ochre grass hill, never flat, its crown higher than the fountain terrace beside it, with a grey stone-slab walkway curving over the rise toward the main building's entrance, a few red-brick paved rectangles set into the grass holding small blank exhibition panels and one red wooden bench, a granite monument stone and a glass display case near tall pines on the left, the long three-storey glass facade of the main building across the back with its elevated bridge wing to the right, and the L-shaped concrete library at the far left behind the pines. Faceless clay figures stroll in ones and twos, one pair taking a photo. Quiet, spacious, breathing room.
