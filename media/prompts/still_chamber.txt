Subject: the chapel as a clay diorama, a SEPARATE building east of the main building, seen from a raised three-quarter angle from the south-west with its roof lifted away. The building is ONE solid rectangular block (a full box plan, never an L-shape, nothing protruding): cream-and-white walls with NO pilotis and NO columns; the tall narrow teal-green glass stair volume and the cream stone tower with a tall cut-out cross stand at the building's south-east corner (front right), the white side block with staggered window boxes on the east side, a glass entrance lobby at the front right beside the tower. The hall fills the inside of the box and is oriented like this: the STAGE is at the FAR (north) end, away from the camera, with the tall slender green cross on that far wall, the pipe organ with red-and-cream pipes at the far-left corner, the grand piano at the far right; all fourteen rows of red-upholstered wooden seats in four blocks with a wide central aisle FACE AWAY from the camera toward that far stage. The NEAR (south) wall, the one closest to the camera, is the hall's rear wall: on its inner face, directly behind the last row of seats, are the entrance doors from the lobby, and above them a shallow mezzanine gallery — a half-level balcony spanning the width of that near wall, slatted light-wood front, a few rows of red seats — whose occupants look over the audience toward the stage; from this angle we see the back of that balcony's parapet and its seats from above. The two long side walls (east and west) carry only the grid of small square windows high up — no doors, no balcony on them. Exposed pale-concrete walls with tie holes, grey stone floor, a wide light-wood stage platform with shallow steps, a narrow stained-glass strip glowing on the west wall. A string quartet of faceless clay figures plays on the stage under warm light; seats mostly filled; two staff in orange crew-neck sweatshirts by the rear doors. Outside, on the WEST side of the chapel toward the main building, the ground is an open paved plaza with NO trees on it — an empty square where a crowd can gather — and at the far left edge of the frame the end of the main building's fountain terrace (a corner of dark pool and a white column) just peeks into view. Pines and autumn trees only behind and to the east; a small forecourt with a few cars at the front right.
