Subject: the chapel as a clay diorama seen from the front at a slightly raised angle, its roof lifted away to reveal the interior: outside, the low white colonnaded porch, the tall narrow teal-green glass stair volume, the cream stone tower with a tall cut-out cross, and the white block with staggered protruding window boxes; pines and a small forecourt in front. Inside, exposed pale concrete walls, a granite floor, rows of warm light-wood pews, long bands of afternoon light falling diagonally across the floor from the glass wall, and at the far end a plain timber platform where a string quartet of faceless clay figures sits with cello, violins and music stands. Intimate, focused, warm.
