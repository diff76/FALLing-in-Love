Rules that apply everywhere: event staff are faceless clay figures wearing orange crew-neck sweatshirts (round-neck, no hood) — put two or three of them wherever guests are received (tent, desk, bus door, stage side); guests wear ordinary autumn clothes. Any bus or car is Korean: the passenger door of the shuttle bus is on the RIGHT side of the vehicle, so the bus stands with its right side toward the pavement or the welcome desk and people board from that side. No text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere.
