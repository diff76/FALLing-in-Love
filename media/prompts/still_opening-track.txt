Subject: the departure point at the Changdong church as a clay diorama from a raised three-quarter street angle. The church is a modern four-storey block clad in dark charcoal-grey corrugated metal, with one large glazed band of tall windows across its upper storey, a plain sign band over the ground floor and a glass-fronted ground-floor café entrance with planters. The building's west end is a pilotis: an open ground floor on slim columns used as a small car park, with a little dark security booth at its edge. Under that pilotis a cream event tent lounge has been set up — light-wood tables and chairs, a small welcome desk with a stack of cards, potted plants and warm string lights under the concrete ceiling — where faceless clay figures in autumn coats wait with coffee. On the pavement directly in front, at the kerb, a small tour bus waits with its door open and a short line of figures boarding: the bus is festive and a little fancy — glossy cream body with a sweeping coral, amber and mint stripe along its side, small pennant flags on the roof corners, warm lights on, like a special event shuttle, tasteful not cartoonish. City street with a bike lane, a street tree in persimmon leaves, morning-to-noon light. No text, no letters on the sign band or the bus.
