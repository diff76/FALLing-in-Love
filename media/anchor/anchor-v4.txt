Subject: bird's-eye clay diorama of a small hillside seminary campus in autumn, high three-quarter aerial view from the south looking north, about 50 degrees down, the whole event world in one frame. Follow the real campus in the reference photographs and map.

TERRAIN (important): the campus is one continuous hillside with NO retaining walls, NO stacked terraces and NO stone steps between levels — every change of height is a natural grassy or wooded slope. Lowest level at the south-east: the bare-earth sports ground used as a car park. Highest ground: a soft, rounded grassy hill in the west-centre (The Trail lawn) whose crown clearly sits higher than the fountain terrace beside it; its top is gently domed and rolling, never flat, and it slopes down naturally through trees and shrubs on all sides. The fountain terrace in front of the main building sits lower than that hill's crown.

At the far south-east corner a small dark gatehouse with barrier arms. At the lowest level the large bare-earth sports ground with a few neat rows of small cars, edged by a plain iron fence. Along its east edge a grey paver strip with one cream peaked welcome tent, a small desk and a cream shuttle bus. Between the sports ground and that welcome strip, a forest stair of dark wooden sleepers set straight into the earth — no paving, no handrails — begins and winds up the wooded slope in a gentle S-curve, passing between pines and rounded autumn shrubs, and arrives on the upper level in the gap between the chapel and the main building.

Buildings, from east to west across the upper ground: the chapel at the east end — a low white colonnaded porch, a tall narrow teal-green glass stair volume, a cream stone tower with a tall cut-out cross, and a white block with staggered protruding window boxes — standing slightly FORWARD (south) of the main building so its front projects ahead of the main building's colonnade line, never behind it. West of the chapel the long modernist main building of pale stone and teal glass curtain wall with an elevated glass bridge wing; in front of it a colonnade of tall white round columns around a rectangular dark reflecting pool with reeds and small fountain jets, brick-red pavers, café tables with cream and amber parasols and a small jazz trio with a half-circle of listeners. West of the terrace the rounded grassy hill of The Trail, dressed as a garden party: a grey stone-slab path curving over its crown toward the main building's entrance; small wooden easels holding blank picture boards and a few slim standing X-banners (blank) placed along the path; clusters of cream, coral and butter-yellow balloons tied to slim posts; strings of small bunting flags and festoon lights on slender poles; a couple of picnic blankets and a small refreshment cart; pines; NO monument stone, NO stele, NO gravestone-like object anywhere. Behind the hill to the north-west the L-shaped concrete library. Dense autumn woodland climbs the hillside all around. No other buildings. No spire, no steeple, no stained glass, no crowds, no stage truss, no walls, no monument stones.
