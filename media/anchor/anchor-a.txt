Subject: bird's-eye clay diorama of a small hillside seminary campus in autumn, high three-quarter aerial view from the south looking north, about 50 degrees down, the whole event world in one frame, matching the composition of the first reference illustration. Follow the real campus in the reference photographs: at the far south-east corner a small dark gatehouse with barrier arms; at the lowest level a large bare-earth sports ground used as a car park with a few neat rows of small cars, edged by a plain iron fence; along its east edge a grey paver strip with one cream peaked welcome tent, a small desk and a shuttle bus; from that strip a short, narrow wooden-sleeper stair without handrails climbs north through pines and shrubs and ends at the chapel's front. The chapel stands at the east end of the building row: a low white colonnaded porch, then a tall narrow teal-green glass stair volume, then a cream stone tower with a tall cut-out cross, then a white block with staggered protruding window boxes. West of the chapel the long modernist main building of pale stone and teal glass curtain wall, with an elevated glass bridge wing; in front of it, one level below the lawn, a colonnade of tall white round columns around a rectangular dark reflecting pool with reeds and small fountain jets, brick-red pavers, café tables with cream and amber parasols and a small jazz trio with a half-circle of listeners. West and above that, a wide flat lawn on the upper plateau with a grey stone-slab walkway leading to the main entrance, a few red-brick paved rectangles holding small exhibition panels, a granite monument stone and pines, and behind it to the north-west the L-shaped concrete library. The campus sits in dense autumn woodland climbing the hillside. No other buildings. No spire, no steeple, no stained glass, no crowds, no stage truss.
